# Scene textures and shaders

The scene textures and shaders listed below use MIT license. All ROV-01 vehicle geometry (`rov01_mesh.js`) is 100% self-designed procedural Three.js geometry with zero external commercial 3D meshes.

| Asset | Source | Author | License | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `waternormals.jpg` | [three.js examples](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r160/examples/textures) | three.js authors | MIT | Surface water normal map |
| `Water.js` / `Sky.js` | `three/addons/objects/` | three.js authors | MIT | Water and sky shaders |

All ROV-01 open-frame industrial vehicle meshes, buoyancy blocks, tubular crash frame, Kort-nozzle thrusters, UT thickness gauge probe, and DVL sensors are generated procedurally at runtime via `viewer/rov01_mesh.js`.
