# Privacy Policy — Draft

> **Draft — legal review required.** This document is for store-prep only. It is not an in-force privacy policy. Replace with a counsel-approved version before shipping.

**Product:** ROV Solution  
**Language:** English  
**Last updated (draft):** 2026-11-24

## 1. Summary

- The game itself **does not collect personal data**.
- Apart from **Steam platform** features, the game **does not phone home** to developer servers.
- Saves and settings live **on your device** and may sync via **Steam Cloud** when enabled.

## 2. What the game stores locally

The client is primarily offline / local. It may store on your device:

- Progress, settings, collections and replay / feedback archives (if you use those features)
- Technical local logs (for example bug-report packages) designed without personal identifiers

Those files stay on your device by default. The game does not create a developer account or upload them to developer-operated servers for that purpose.

## 3. Steam and platform services

If you play through Steam, Valve / Steam may process account, purchase, achievement, cloud-save and other platform data under Steam’s own privacy policy. That processing is Steam’s responsibility and is outside what this game collects itself. Please also read the Steam privacy policy and subscriber agreement.

## 4. Networking

- **Game client:** Steam features use Steam services. The internal edition also supports multiplayer over a local network.
- **Optional multiplayer / self-hosted servers:** if you enter a server address and join a room, the endpoint is chosen by you; a public relay is not a shipping commitment unless stated in the release notes.
- **Runtime AI:** pre-made voice and assets are **not** regenerated by calling external AI APIs at runtime.

## 5. AI-generated content

Radio voice lines and some third-party fish textures involve AI generation as pre-made shipped assets; see `CREDITS.md` and the store AI disclosure. That pipeline is not personal-data collection from players.

## 6. Children

This product is not directed at children. Where local law adds rules for minors, a guardian should decide whether use is allowed and manage the Steam account accordingly.

## 7. Your choices

- Manage cloud sync in the OS or Steam settings.
- Delete local saves and report files from the application user-data folder.
- Account requests about Steam should go through Steam / Valve channels.

## 8. Contact

Publisher contact: to be filled in by counsel / the shipping owner.

## 9. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be noted on the store page or in patch notes. Continued use after an update means you acknowledge the revised notice (final legal effect wording TBD by counsel).
