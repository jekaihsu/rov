### 完整說明

#### 你的工作，在鏡頭另一端

ROV Solution 是以真實操作為核心的水下遙控載具模擬遊戲。你是 YX 公司的新進 ROV 駕駛員，從水池練習走向港口、船殼與養殖場。看簡報、檢查設備、規劃路線，再把能交付的觀測結果帶回來。

#### 推力、纜線與水流，都是操作的一部分

手動操作時，推桿指令代表推力；鬆桿不會讓載具瞬間停住。洋流會推移載具，纜線會施力，也會限制你繞過結構的路線。透過機載第一人稱鏡頭控制距離、姿態與退路，完成一次有把握的接近。

有限能見度、影像延遲與感測器誤差會影響判讀。定點需要有效的 DVL 感測資料，也受可用推力限制。操作輔助與第三人稱訓練視角可供練習；關閉輔助後，面對的是同一套物理模型。模型仍在校準中，不代表任何特定真機的完整複製。

## English (english)

### Short description

Work as a YX ROV pilot. Handle thrust, tether forces, currents and sensor errors as you inspect underwater structures and deliver evidence. Practice the procedures behind each dive. The first Early Access release is planned to include a prologue and two chapters, with around 10 missions.

### Full description

#### Your work is on the other side of the camera

ROV Solution is an underwater remotely operated vehicle simulator focused on realistic operation. Join YX as a new ROV pilot, progressing from pool practice to harbors, ship hulls and aquaculture sites. Read the briefing, check your equipment, plan your route and bring back useful observations.

#### Thrust, tether and current are part of the job

In manual control, stick input commands thrust; releasing it does not stop the vehicle instantly. Currents move the vehicle, while the tether exerts force and constrains your route around structures. Use the onboard first-person camera to manage distance, attitude and a clear way back.

#### Readings require judgment

Limited visibility, video delay and sensor errors affect your decisions. Position holding requires valid DVL sensor data and remains limited by available thrust. Optional control assists and a third-person training view help you practice; turning them off leaves the same underlying physics. The model is still being calibrated and is not a complete replica of any specific real vehicle.

#### From procedure to delivery

The first release is planned around dive preparation, inspection, photography and measurement, recovery and debriefing. Work quality, coverage, tether management and collision records matter more than simply rushing. YX's in-game qualifications are progression goals, not professional credentials.

#### Early Access scope

The first release is planned to contain a pool-training prologue, Chapter One: Harbors and Vessels, and Chapter Two: Aquaculture, totaling around 10 missions. The game is not yet available to buy; mission details and supported features will be updated against release testing before sales begin. Public-service, offshore-wind, subsea-cable and advanced work-class chapters are outside this initial scope. A complete career, online multiplayer, VR, Workshop, Steam Deck verification and full controller support are not current feature commitments on this page.

We aim to provide interfaces and subtitles in Traditional Chinese, English, Simplified Chinese and Japanese. Radio voices use AI speech synthesis. Content, balance and save formats may change during Early Access; updates affecting older saves will be explained in the patch notes.

### Features

- First-person onboard piloting, manual thrust control and optional training assists.
- A force-bearing tether, currents and limited visibility.
- Navigation decisions shaped by sensor errors and validity limits.
- Underwater work from pre-dive procedures to delivery of observation evidence.
- First-release target: a prologue and two chapters, around 10 missions; YX employment and fictional qualifications.

We do not yet have a reliable end date. Timing depends on mission production and validation of controls and save stability. We will update this information as development progresses. Please base purchases on the content available at the time, rather than a guarantee of future additions.

**How is the full version planned to differ from the Early Access version?**
We plan to expand into public-service, offshore-wind, subsea-cable and advanced operations, alongside improvements to tutorials, interface and stability. These are development directions; specific chapters and features may change and are not a guaranteed delivery list.

**What is the current state of the Early Access version?**
The game is in release preparation and is not yet available to buy. Our first purchasable release targets a prologue and Chapters One and Two, around 10 missions covering pool training, harbors and vessels, and aquaculture. Steam integration and full playthrough validation remain pending. Before sales begin, this answer will be updated to describe the content actually available in the release candidate.

**Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access?**
Pricing and future adjustments have not been finalized. We will assess the price against delivered content and announce changes in advance through Steam.

**How are you planning on involving the Community in your development process?**
We plan to collect reports and suggestions through Steam Discussions and document fixes in patch notes. Please include the mission, game version, input device, realism settings and reproduction steps. Feedback on handling, terminology, tutorials and scoring will help set priorities; not every suggestion will be implemented.

### 詳細説明

#### カメラの向こうが、あなたの現場

ROV Solutionは、実際の操作を重視した水中遠隔操作機のシミュレーターです。YX社の新人ROVパイロットとして、プールでの練習から港湾、船体、養殖場へと進みます。作業説明を読み、装備を点検し、経路を考え、提出できる観測記録を持ち帰りましょう。

#### 推力、テザー、海流を扱う

手動操作では、スティック入力が推力を指示します。手を離しても機体は瞬時には止まりません。海流は機体を押し流し、テザーは力を及ぼして構造物の周囲で取れる経路を制限します。機上カメラの一人称視点から、距離、姿勢、退路を確かめて接近します。

#### 数値を読むだけでは足りない

限られた視界、映像の遅延、センサー誤差が判断に影響します。定点保持には有効なDVLデータが必要で、利用できる推力にも限界があります。操作アシストと三人称の訓練視点を使って練習し、必要に応じてオフにできます。基礎となる物理モデルは共通です。モデルは調整中であり、特定の実機を完全に再現するものではありません。

#### 手順を踏み、成果を提出する

初版では、潜航準備、点検、撮影と計測、回収、講評という作業の流れを予定しています。速さだけでなく、作業品質、点検範囲、テザー管理、衝突記録が重要です。YXのゲーム内資格は進行目標であり、実社会の職業資格ではありません。

初版はプール訓練のプロローグ、第1章「港湾と船舶」、第2章「養殖場」の約10ミッションを予定しています。現在は購入できません。発売前に、検証結果に基づいて収録ミッションと対応機能を更新します。公共サービス、洋上風力、海底ケーブル、高度な作業級ミッションの章は初版に含みません。全キャリア、オンラインマルチプレイ、VR、Workshop、Steam Deck認証、フルコントローラサポートは、このページで提供済みとする機能ではありません。
